Skills & Qualifications

Core competencies, software mastery, and industry-recognized academic qualifications.

Core Graphic Design Competencies

Visual Identity & Branding

Crafting memorable brand identities, logo marks, color systems, typography pairs, and comprehensive brand style guidelines for multi-platform consistency.

Logo design • Brand books

Typography & Layout Design

Deep mastery of typesetting, kerning, leading, hierarchy, baseline grids, and editorial layout systems for print magazines, books, and digital media.

Micro-typography • Grid systems

UI/UX & Wireframing

Designing user-centric digital interfaces, wireframes, interactive prototypes, design systems, and responsive website layouts tailored for conversion.

Component libraries • Prototyping

Motion Graphics & Animation

Bringing vector artwork and typography to life with smooth keyframe animation, title sequences, logo reveals, and social video assets.

Kinetic type • Logo animatics

Print & Prepress Packaging

Expert knowledge of CMYK color spaces, bleed margins, spot varnish, dielines, foil stamping, paper stock selection, and print proof approval.

CMYK / Pantone • Dielines

Vector Illustration & Art

Creating scalable custom vector graphics, icons, character art, infographics, and promotional advertising creative from scratch.

Custom icons • SVG Artwork

Section 02

Core Duties & Workflow

Essential responsibilities carried out from initial client brief through to final artwork delivery.

01

Client Briefing & Discovery

Analyzing creative briefs, establishing project scope, target demographic research, competitor audits, and clarifying key messaging goals.

Discovery & Requirements
02

Concepting & Moodboarding

Curating visual moodboards, sketching rough thumbnails, exploring typography combinations, and defining creative direction options.

Conceptual Exploration
03

Brand Identity System Creation

Vectorizing primary logo marks, sub-marks, iconography, color palettes, and compiling comprehensive brand style guideline books.

Style Guides & Systems
04

Digital & Social Media Assets

Designing high-converting digital ad banners, social media templates, email newsletters, and animated GIF/MP4 promotional graphics.

Marketing & Campaign Assets
05

Print Layout & Prepress

Preparing press-ready PDFs with crop marks, bleeds, vector paths, CMYK color profiles, and approving printer proof copies.

Press-Ready Packaging
06

UI/UX Wireframing & Prototype

Constructing screen layouts in Figma, establishing interactive clickable prototypes, and handing off design tokens to developers.

Web & App UI Handoff
Section 03

Average Pay & Salary Tiers

Industry standard salary benchmarks and freelance day rates (UK Market context).

Entry / Junior Level

£22k – £28k

Per Annum (UK Avg)

0–2 years experience. Focuses on asset generation, resizing, formatting, following existing brand books under senior supervision.

Day rate: ~£120 – £180/day
Popular
Mid-Level Designer

£28k – £38k

Per Annum (UK Avg)

2–5 years experience. Independently manages project briefs, client communications, branding initiatives, and complex print/web campaigns.

Day rate: ~£200 – £320/day
Senior Designer

£38k – £52k

Per Annum (UK Avg)

5+ years experience. Leads design strategy, presents to major clients, oversees junior designers, and manages design systems.

Day rate: ~£350 – £450/day
Creative Director

£55k – £80k+

Per Annum (Executive)

Executive level agency or in-house lead. Oversees full studio direction, client retention, marketing vision, and team scaling.

Day rate: ~£500 – £800+/day
Note: London & Southeast UK rates typically carry a 15% – 25% regional premium compared to national averages.
